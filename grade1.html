📝 Sindh Board Grade 1 Practice Guide

Key Learning Goals for Grade 1 (Sindh Board):

  • English: Recognize and write small & capital letters A-Z, phonetic sounds, 3-letter sight words (Cat, Dog, Sun, Bag).
  • Sindhi (سنڌي سليس): Master Sindhi alphabet (الف، ب، ٻ، ڀ، ت...), recognize basic words like اٺ (Camel), ٻلي (Cat), گل (Flower).
  • Mathematics: Count 1 to 20 with objects, solve simple additions (2+3=5), simple subtractions (5-2=3), identify shapes (Circle, Triangle, Square).
  • Science & GK: 5 senses (Eyes, Ears, Nose, Mouth, Hands), identify farm/wild animals, parts of a plant, traffic light colors (Red, Yellow, Green).
💡 Tip for parents: Encourage your child to practice 15-20 minutes a day and earn stars on EduNexa AI!
