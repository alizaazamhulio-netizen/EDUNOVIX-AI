Think of the Krebs Cycle like a molecular energy factory:

  • 📍 Where: Mitochondrial Matrix (Eukaryotes).
  • ⚡ Input: Acetyl CoA (2 carbons) binds to Oxaloacetate (4 carbons) → Citrate.
  • 🎯 Key Output per Glucose (2 turns): 6 NADH, 2 FADH₂, 2 ATP, 4 CO₂.
