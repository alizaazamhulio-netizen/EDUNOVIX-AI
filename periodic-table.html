🧪 Lab 1: Period Radius Contraction vs. Nuclear Charge (Zeff)

Visualize how increasing nuclear charge pulls the electron cloud tighter across a period.

Select Period:
Period 3: Na (186pm) ➔ Ar (97pm) — Radius DECREASES across period.

⭐ Why? Electrons are added into the same valence shell (n=3) while protons increase by +1 at each step, causing Effective Nuclear Charge (Zeff) to rise and pull electron shells inward.

🧪 Lab 2: Group Shell Expansion & Shielding Effect

Observe how adding new principal energy levels increases shielding and decreases ionization energy.

Select Group:
Group 1: Top (Li, IE=520kJ) ➔ Bottom (Cs, IE=376kJ) — Ionization Energy DECREASES.

⭐ Why? Each step down a group adds a whole new electron shell (increasing n). The inner electrons shield the valence electrons from nuclear pull, making outermost electrons much easier to remove.
