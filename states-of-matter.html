1. What is Matter & The Particle Model

What is Matter?

Matter is anything that has mass, occupies space (volume), and is composed of tiny moving particles.

  • Main States: Solid → Liquid → Gas
  • Special States: Plasma & Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC)
  • For MDCAT, solid, liquid, and gas form the core syllabus.

The Particle Model Rules

  • ✓ Particles are in continuous motion at all temperatures above 0 Kelvin.
  • ✓ Particles have empty spaces between them.
  • ✓ Particles exert intermolecular attractive forces on each other.
  • ✓ When temperature rises, particles move faster and kinetic energy increases.

Why Evaporation Causes Cooling

Particles with higher kinetic energy escape from the liquid surface. The average kinetic energy of the remaining particles drops, lowering the liquid's temperature!

FACTORS INCREASING EVAPORATION
↑ Higher Temperature • ↑ Larger Surface Area • ↑ Higher Wind Speed • ↓ Lower Humidity • ↓ Weaker Intermolecular Forces

7. Diffusion & Graham's Law of Diffusion Lab

Graham's Law Formula

Rate₁ / Rate₂ = √(M₂ / M₁)

Simple takeaway: Lighter gas particles move faster at the same temperature, so they diffuse much faster than heavy gases.

⭐
Classic MDCAT Question: Hydrogen ($H_2$, M=2) diffuses 4 times faster than Oxygen ($O_2$, M=32) because $\sqrt{32/2} = \sqrt{16} = 4$.

NEAR 0 KELVIN
🌀 Super-Atom Quantum Wave

Formed at temperatures extremely close to Absolute Zero (0 K / -273.15°C). Individual atoms clump together to act as a single giant "super-atom".

🚨 10 Critical MDCAT Traps & Common Pitfalls

Click any card to reveal the exam trap breakdown and simple trick to remember it:

TRAP 1 Evaporation vs. Boiling are NOT the same
Students frequently confuse surface evaporation with bulk liquid boiling.
Key Difference: Evaporation happens only at the liquid surface at any temperature below BP. Boiling occurs throughout the bulk liquid strictly at the boiling point.
TRAP 2 Solid Particles are NOT completely motionless
Do solid particles stand completely still?
Correction: Solid particles continually vibrate around fixed lattice positions. They only theoretically stop moving at Absolute Zero (0 Kelvin).
TRAP 3 Phase Changes Occur at CONSTANT Temperature
Does water get hotter while boiling at 100°C?
Key Fact: No! During melting and boiling, temperature remains constant because heat energy is used as latent heat to break intermolecular bonds.
TRAP 4 Gas Law Temperatures MUST be in Kelvin
Plugging Celsius directly into $V_1/T_1 = V_2/T_2$ yields wrong answers.
Always Convert: $T(\text{K}) = T(^\circ\text{C}) + 273$. Example: $27^\circ\text{C} = 300\text{ K}$, $127^\circ\text{C} = 400\text{ K}$.
TRAP 5 Gas Pressure is Wall Collisions, NOT Particle-to-Particle
What physically causes gas pressure?
Pressure Cause: Gas pressure is the force per unit area produced when particles collide with the walls of the container.
TRAP 6 Hydrogen Diffuses 4× Faster than Oxygen, NOT 16×
Remember Graham's square root relationship!
Calculation: $M(O_2)/M(H_2) = 32/2 = 16$. The square root $\sqrt{16} = 4$. So $H_2$ diffuses 4 times faster.
TRAP 7 Dry Ice Sublimes Directly to Gas
Does dry ice melt into a liquid puddle?
Fact: Dry Ice is solid Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$). Under atmospheric pressure, it undergoes sublimation directly into gas without leaving any liquid residue.
TRAP 8 Gases are Highly Compressible; Liquids & Solids are Not
Why can a massive volume of gas fit in a small cylinder?
Reason: More than 99.9% of a gas's volume is empty space between widely separated particles.
