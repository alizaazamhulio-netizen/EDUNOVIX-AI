🇵🇰

National Symbols of Pakistan

  • Founder: Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  • National Poet: Allama Muhammad Iqbal
  • National Animal: Markhor 🐐
  • National Flower: Jasmine (چمبيلي) 🌸
  • National Fruit: Mango (آم) 🥭
  • National Game: Field Hockey 🏑
🏛️

Wonders of Sindh

  • Mohenjo-daro: Ancient 5000-year-old Indus Valley city near Larkana!
  • Keenjhar Lake: Beautiful freshwater lake in Thatta.
  • Ajrak & Sindhi Topi: Traditional cultural symbols of honor & pride.
  • Mazar-e-Quaid: Peaceful mausoleum of Quaid-e-Azam in Karachi.
👨‍🚒

Our Community Helpers

  • Teacher: Helps us read, write, and grow wise 📚
  • Doctor & Nurse: Care for us when we are sick 🩺
  • Farmer: Grows delicious wheat, rice, and vegetables 🌾
  • Police Officer: Keeps our city safe and peaceful 👮

Traffic Light Rules

  • 🔴 Red Light: STOP immediately!
  • 🟡 Yellow Light: GET READY and slow down!
  • 🟢 Green Light: GO safely!
  • 🦓 Zebra Crossing: Always cross the road here holding hands with an adult.

Sami's Little Garden

Sami loved spending mornings in his grandmother's garden in Hyderabad. Every day before school, he watered the yellow sunflowers and red rose bushes. One sunny morning, he noticed a tiny green caterpillar munching on a leaf. A few days later, the caterpillar transformed into a gorgeous blue butterfly! Sami smiled and promised to plant more sweet flower seeds.

Comprehension Question:

Where was the garden located?

🦜 Animal Tale
🦜 🥭 🌳

The Clever Parrot and the Mango

High on a tall green mango tree lived Mithu, a cheerful green parrot with a bright red beak. The mangoes were ripe, sweet, and golden. One afternoon, a grumpy crow tried to chase Mithu away. Mithu spotted a shiny red balloon on the ground, popped it with his beak—BANG! The loud noise scared the crow away, and Mithu happily shared the sweet Sindhri mangoes with his friend Zara.

Comprehension Question:

What kind of mangoes did Mithu eat?

🌊 Adventure Story
🚤 🌊 🦩

A Trip to Keenjhar Lake

During the summer holidays, Ali and his family traveled to Keenjhar Lake in Thatta. The lake water was sparkling blue and calm. They saw graceful white herons and pink flamingos resting near the water banks. Ali took a thrilling motorboat ride wearing his orange safety life jacket. He learned that Keenjhar is one of Pakistan's largest freshwater lakes.

Comprehension Question:

What safety gear did Ali wear on the boat?
