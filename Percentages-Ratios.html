Unit 1 • Fundamentals

Percentages & Equivalent Representations

What is a Percentage?

Per Cent = Per 100

A percentage is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. It is denoted using the percent sign %. Because it uses a standard base of 100, percentages make comparing proportions across different sample sizes effortless.

50% = 50100 = 12 = 0.50
75% = 75100 = 34 = 0.75

Unit 2 • Ratio Analysis

Understanding & Manipulating Ratios

What is a Ratio?

Comparative Magnitude

A ratio indicates how many times one number contains another. It compares two quantities of the same unit, written as a : b or a / b.

Ratio Notation: 2 : 3 ("2 to 3") = 23 ≈ 0.667

Visual representation: In a fruit basket with 2 apples for every 3 oranges, the ratio of apples to oranges is 2:3. The total parts are 2 + 3 = 5 parts.

Proportions & Cross Multiplication

What is a Proportion?

Equality of Two Ratios

A proportion is a mathematical statement asserting that two ratios or fractions are equal. If a / b = c / d, then the cross products are always equal: a × d = b × c.

ab = cd ⟺ a · d = b · c

Quick Reference

Formula Cheat Sheet

Percentage Formula
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100
Calculates the base-100 share of a total sum.
Percentage of a Number
Value = (Percentage × Number) / 100
e.g. 20% of 150 = (20 × 150) / 100 = 30
Percentage Change
% Change = ((New − Orig) / Orig) × 100
Positive indicates increase; negative indicates decrease.
Ratio Simplification
(a / GCD) : (b / GCD)
Divide both terms by their Greatest Common Divisor.
Cross Multiplication
a / b = c / d  ⟹  a · d = b · c
The product of the extremes equals the product of the means.
Profit & Loss
Profit = SP − CP  |  Loss = CP − SP
% Profit/Loss = (Profit or Loss / Cost Price) × 100

Worked Examples

Example 1
Find 35% of 240.

Formula: (Percentage / 100) × Total

Substitution: (35 / 100) × 240

Calculation: 0.35 × 240 = 84

Answer: 84

Example 2
A shirt costs $80 and is discounted by 25%. Find the final price.

Discount Amount: $80 × 0.25 = $20

Final Price: $80 − $20 = $60

Answer: $60.00

Example 3
Simplify the ratio 45 : 60.

GCD Calculation: Factors of 45 and 60 give GCD = 15.

Term Division: 45 ÷ 15 = 3, 60 ÷ 15 = 4.

Answer: 3 : 4

Example 4
Solve for x in the proportion: 3 / 5 = x / 20.

Cross Multiply: 5 · x = 3 · 20

Simplify Equation: 5x = 60

Solve for x: x = 60 / 5 = 12

Answer: x = 12
