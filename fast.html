Financial Aid & Merit Support

FAST Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Comprehensive guide to academic merit awards, interest-free study loans, provincial endowments, and alumni assistance funds.

Academic Excellence

Merit Scholarship

Recognizes outstanding academic achievers at admission and rewards continuous academic brilliance throughout the degree.

  • Top three position holders of each Examination Board in the year of admission may receive the merit scholarship.
  • Top three students in the NU admission merit list of each campus may also receive the merit scholarship.
  • The scholarship can cover full tuition under the stated FAST university conditions.
  • Continuation requires maintaining a semester GPA of at least 3.0 and full course-load requirements.
Check Official Scholarship Rules ↗
Need-Based Assistance

FAST Interest-Free Study Loan

FAST ensures that deserving students with genuine financial constraints are not deprived of higher education due to affordability.

  • Need-based financial assistance provided as an interest-free study loan.
  • Application through the prescribed financial assistance process with verified documentation.
  • Both academic performance and verified family financial need are considered by the aid committee.
  • Repayment conditions apply according to agreed post-graduation terms (Note: This is an interest-free loan, not a free gift grant).
Study Loan Information ↗
Provincial Major Endowment

Sindh Government Endowment Board Scholarship

A high-impact need-cum-merit financial award program supporting talented students domicile holders of Sindh province across FAST campuses.

  • Need-cum-merit based support for undergraduate and graduate levels in all disciplines.
  • FAST states that it covers full tuition for the program duration, renewable yearly under applicable rules.
  • Rural Quota: 60% reserved for applicants from rural districts of Sindh.
  • Urban Quota: 40% reserved for applicants from urban centers of Sindh.

Additional External & Alumni Financial Assistance

Deserving students may also qualify for regional endowments, alumni trusts, and community foundations:

PEEF Scholarship

Punjab Educational Endowment Fund provides merit-and-need support to eligible domicile holders under applicable PEEF guidelines.

Punjab Honhaar Scholarship

Provincial youth support initiative; availability and eligibility depend on the relevant active government program terms.

ICT Scholarships

National ICT R&D / Ignite initiatives offering tuition support for computer science & engineering talent.

Balochistan Endowment

BEEF financial awards designated for eligible domicile students pursuing higher education across accredited universities.

OSAF Scholarship

FAST's Old Students Association provides financial assistance to students facing hardship who cannot afford the full fee.

FANA Scholarship

FAST Alumni of North America provides financial assistance for deserving students pursuing computing and technology degrees.

Ihsan Trust

Offers interest-free Qarz-e-Hasna facility to assist deserving students who genuinely cannot afford their semester fee.

Community Scholarships

Includes Memon Community, Bohra Community, and Aga Khan Foundation financial support schemes for eligible applicants.

External Funding Notice: External scholarship rules are controlled by the relevant organization. Always verify the latest eligibility, deadlines and application procedure directly from the official provider.
