INORGANIC ESSENTIAL • CELLULAR MEDIUM

Water — The Foundation of Life

Water is the most abundant and indispensable inorganic substance in living organisms, typically accounting for 70% to 85% of total cellular mass. Due to its polar covalent nature and extensive hydrogen bonding capacity, water provides an ideal chemical milieu for biochemical reactions.

Water Molecule Polarity & Hydrogen Bonding Network MDCAT CONCEPT

Polarity of H₂O allows each water molecule to form up to four hydrogen bonds with neighboring molecules, generating critical cohesive forces, high surface tension, and exceptional thermal stability.

💧 COHESION

Attraction between Water Molecules: Water molecules cling to one another due to hydrogen bonding. Generates high surface tension and continuous fluid columns in plant xylem transport.

🧲 ADHESION

Attraction between Water & Other Polar Surfaces: Water molecules adhere to polar/charged surfaces (such as cellulosic cell walls), facilitating capillary action.

High Specific Heat

Water absorbs or loses significant heat energy with very little change in temperature, acting as a thermal buffer for cells and ecosystems.

High Heat of Vaporization

Massive amounts of heat are absorbed during liquid-to-vapor phase transition, enabling effective evaporative cooling (perspiration & transpiration).

Universal Biological Solvent

Forms hydration shells around ions and polar biomolecules, keeping solutes dispersed for enzyme catalyzed metabolic reactions.

⚠️

MDCAT Trap Alert: Cohesion vs Adhesion

Cohesion is strictly water-to-water attraction. Adhesion is water-to-other-surface attraction. Questions often invert these definitions regarding xylem ascent of sap!

01 — CARBOHYDRATES • ENERGY • STORAGE • STRUCTURE

Carbohydrates (Hydrates of Carbon)

Carbohydrates are polyhydroxy aldehydes or ketones (or substances yielding them on hydrolysis). They are composed primarily of Carbon, Hydrogen, and Oxygen with the general empirical formula Cn(H2O)n (approximate C:H:O ≈ 1:2:1 ratio, though derivatives such as deoxyribose and amino sugars deviate).

1. Monosaccharides (Simple Monomer Sugars)

Sweet, crystalline, soluble in water, and cannot be hydrolyzed into smaller carbohydrate subunits.

🧪 Glucose

Aldohexose (C₆H₁₂O₆). Primary cellular fuel for glycolysis & cellular respiration; blood sugar.

🍎 Fructose

Ketohexose (C₆H₁₂O₆). Sweetest natural sugar found abundantly in ripe fruits and honey.

🥛 Galactose

Aldohexose (C₆H₁₂O₆). Structural epimer of glucose; core component of milk disaccharide lactose.

🧬 Ribose & Deoxyribose

Aldopentoses (5 carbons). Ribose (C₅H₁₀O₅) in RNA & ATP; Deoxyribose (C₅H₁₀O₄) in DNA (lacks oxygen at 2' carbon).

2. Disaccharides & Glycosidic Bond Formation

Two monosaccharides joined by a covalent glycosidic bond formed via condensation (dehydration) with the release of one water molecule.

Glucose + Fructose
=
Sucrose (Cane Sugar)

Non-reducing table sugar; plant transport sugar

Glucose + Galactose
=
Lactose (Milk Sugar)

Major mammalian milk disaccharide (β-1,4 linkage)

Glucose + Glucose
=
Maltose (Malt Sugar)

Intermediate in starch digestion (α-1,4 linkage)

Glycosidic Bond Mechanism: Condensation vs Hydrolysis BIOCHEMICAL REACTION

3. Polysaccharides (Complex Macromolecules)

🌱 Starch

Plant Energy Storage: Polymer of α-glucose. Composed of unbranched helical Amylose (α-1,4) and branched Amylopectin (α-1,4 & α-1,6).

🐾 Glycogen

Animal & Fungal Storage: "Animal starch". Highly branched α-glucose polymer stored extensively in liver and skeletal muscles.

🌿 Cellulose

Plant Cell Walls: Linear β-glucose chains with β-1,4 glycosidic bonds. Forms insoluble microfibrils. Indigestible by human enzymes.

🦗 Chitin

Exoskeletons & Fungi: Structural polymer of N-acetylglucosamine. Tough, lightweight, water-insoluble protective matrix.

⭐

MDCAT High-Yield Summary: Carbohydrates

Plant Storage: Starch | Animal/Fungal Storage: Glycogen | Plant Structure: Cellulose | Arthropod/Fungal Structure: Chitin.

02 — LIPIDS • ENERGY • MEMBRANES • SIGNALING

Lipids (Hydrophobic Biomolecules)

Lipids are a heterogeneous group of organic compounds insoluble in polar solvents (water) but soluble in non-polar organic solvents (ether, chloroform, benzene). Because of higher C-H bond proportion and lower oxygen content, lipids yield more than double the energy per gram (9.3 kcal/g) compared to carbohydrates (4.1 kcal/g).

Saturated Fatty Acids

Contain NO carbon-carbon double bonds (C-C only). Hydrocarbon tails are straight, pack tightly together, have higher melting points, and are generally solid at room temperature (e.g., Palmitic acid, Stearic acid, Animal fats).

Unsaturated Fatty Acids

Contain one or more C=C double bonds introducing rigid kinks/bends. Cannot pack tightly; have lower melting points and remain liquid at room temperature (e.g., Oleic acid, Linoleic acid, Plant oils).
Monounsaturated = 1 double bond | Polyunsaturated = 2+ double bonds

Triglyceride (Neutral Fat) Structure & Ester Bond ESTERIFICATION

Condensation between the carboxyl group of three fatty acids and three hydroxyl groups of one glycerol molecule forms three covalent ester bonds, producing a triglyceride.

Phospholipids: The Architecture of Biological Membranes

Formed when one fatty acid of a triglyceride is replaced by a phosphate group (often bound to nitrogenous choline/ethanolamine).

Hydrophilic Head

Polar, water-soluble, charged phosphate head facing aqueous intracellular and extracellular fluids.

Hydrophobic Tails

Two non-polar fatty acid hydrocarbon chains orienting inward away from water to create a selective permeable barrier.

Steroids & Waxes

Steroids: 4 fused carbon rings (e.g., Cholesterol, Estrogen, Testosterone).
Waxes: High molecular weight fatty acid + long-chain alcohol coatings preventing water loss.

⚠️

MDCAT Exam Trap: Cholesterol is a Lipid

Students often mistake cholesterol for a structural protein due to its presence in cell membranes. Cholesterol is definitively a steroid lipid and serves as the precursor for bile salts, Vitamin D, and steroid hormones.

03 — PROTEINS • STRUCTURE • ENZYMES • MACHINERY

Proteins (Polymers of Amino Acids)

Proteins are the most functionally versatile macromolecules in living organisms, constituting over 50% of the dry weight of most cells. They serve as biocatalysts (enzymes), structural scaffolds (collagen, keratin), transport vehicles (hemoglobin), antibodies (immunoglobulins), and contractile elements (actin, myosin).

General Chemical Anatomy of an Amino Acid 20 STANDARD AMINO ACIDS

The variable R-group (side chain) confers unique polarity, electrical charge, hydrophobicity, and size to each of the 20 standard amino acids.

The Four Hierarchical Levels of Protein Structure

LEVEL 01

Primary

Linear covalent sequence of amino acids in a polypeptide chain. Determined genetically by mRNA codons.

Bond: Peptide Bonds (-CO-NH-)
LEVEL 02

Secondary

Local repetitive folding of the polypeptide backbone into α-helix or β-pleated sheet.

Bond: Backbone Hydrogen Bonds
LEVEL 03

Tertiary

Overall 3D globular conformation of a single polypeptide chain driven by interactions between R-groups.

Bond: Disulfide, Ionic, Hydrophobic, H-Bonds
LEVEL 04

Quaternary

Spatial arrangement of two or more polypeptide subunits to form a functional multimeric complex (e.g. Hemoglobin: 2α + 2β chains).

Bond: Non-covalent subunit interactions

Protein Denaturation vs Primary Sequence Integrity

Denaturation is the physical or chemical disruption of a protein's 3D higher-order conformation (secondary, tertiary, quaternary) resulting in loss of biological function.

Native Folded Protein
🧶

Intact active site & catalytic/structural function.

High Temp / Extreme pH ➔ Denaturation
Denatured Unfolded Chain
〰️

Loss of 3D shape; primary peptide sequence stays intact.

NATIVE STATE: Functional 3D globular conformation stabilized by H-bonds, ionic interactions, & disulfide bridges.

BIOCATALYSIS • KINETICS

Enzymes — Biological Catalysts

Enzymes are specialized globular protein catalysts that accelerate biochemical reaction rates up to millions of times by lowering the Activation Energy (Ea) barrier. They do NOT alter the net free-energy difference (ΔG) or the chemical equilibrium.

🔒 Lock and Key Model (Fischer)

Proposes that the enzyme's active site has a rigid, complementary shape to the substrate, fitting like a key into a lock. (Historical model).

🖐️ Induced-Fit Model (Koshland)

Modern Accepted Model: Substrate binding induces a conformational adjustment in the active site, placing catalytic residues into optimal alignment to strain bonds.

Factors Affecting Enzyme Reaction Rates

1. Temperature

Rate increases with kinetic collisions until thermal denaturation destroys 3D active site.

2. pH Optimum

Extreme pH alters ionization of amino acid R-groups in active site, causing reversible/irreversible denaturation.

3. [Substrate]

Initial linear rise plateaus at Vmax when all enzyme active sites become saturated.

4. [Enzyme]

Directly proportional increase in rate provided excess substrate is available.

04 — NUCLEIC ACIDS • DNA • RNA • GENETICS

Nucleic Acids (DNA & RNA)

Nucleic acids are linear polymers of repeating monomeric units termed nucleotides. They store, transmit, and express hereditary instructions governing cellular protein synthesis and genetic inheritance.

Nucleoside vs Nucleotide Chemical Composition MDCAT CORE

Comprehensive Comparison: DNA vs RNA

Feature DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) RNA (Ribonucleic Acid)
Pentose Sugar Deoxyribose (C₅H₁₀O₄) Ribose (C₅H₁₀O₅)
Nitrogenous Bases Adenine, Thymine, Cytosine, Guanine Adenine, Uracil, Cytosine, Guanine

ENERGY CURRENCY

ATP — Adenosine Triphosphate

ATP is the universal chemical energy currency of all living cells. Chemically, it is a specialized ribonucleotide composed of the nitrogenous base Adenine, a Ribose sugar, and a chain of Three phosphate groups joined by high-energy phosphoanhydride bonds.

ATP + H₂O ➔ ADP + Pi + Energy (~7.3 kcal/mol / 30.5 kJ/mol)

Hydrolysis of the terminal phosphoanhydride bond relieves electrostatic repulsion among negatively charged oxygens, liberating usable free energy.

Active Transport

Pumping ions against electrochemical gradients (e.g. Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase pump in neurons).

Muscle Contraction

Drives myosin head cross-bridge cycling and actin filament sliding in muscle fibers.

Biosynthesis

Supplies energy for endergonic condensation reactions (peptide bonds, DNA replication).

Signal Transduction

Substrate for protein kinases and adenylyl cyclase (generating second messenger cAMP).

EXAM PITFALLS

⚠️ Don't Fall For These (15 MDCAT Traps)

Examiners specifically construct distractors around subtle biochemical distinctions. Memorize these direct comparisons:

TRAP 01 #1
❌ "Starch is animal storage"
Truth: Starch is exclusively plant storage; Glycogen is animal/fungal storage.
TRAP 02 #2
❌ "Cellulose is an energy storage carb"
Truth: Cellulose is purely structural in plant cell walls; humans cannot hydrolyze its β-1,4 bonds.
TRAP 03 #3
❌ "DNA has Ribose sugar"
Truth: DNA has Deoxyribose (lacks 2'-OH); RNA has Ribose.
TRAP 04 #4
❌ "RNA contains Thymine"
Truth: RNA uses Uracil (U) instead of Thymine (T).
TRAP 05 #5
❌ "Nucleosides have phosphate"
Truth: Nucleoside = Sugar + Base. Nucleotide = Sugar + Base + Phosphate.
TRAP 06 #6
❌ "Triglycerides have 2 fatty acids"
Truth: Triglycerides have 3 fatty acids. Phospholipids have 2 fatty acids + 1 phosphate group.
TRAP 07 #7
❌ "Primary structure is 3D folded shape"
Truth: Primary structure is strictly the linear amino acid sequence (peptide bonds only).
TRAP 08 #8
❌ "Enzymes alter overall reaction ΔG"
Truth: Enzymes strictly lower Activation Energy (Ea); they do not change ΔG or equilibrium.
TRAP 09 #9
❌ "Denaturation cleaves peptide bonds"
Truth: Denaturation disrupts higher-order 3D folding; primary peptide bonds usually stay intact.
TRAP 10 #10
❌ "Cholesterol is a membrane protein"
Truth: Cholesterol is a steroid lipid containing four fused carbon rings.
TRAP 11 #11
❌ "All biological catalysts are proteins"
Truth: While most enzymes are proteins, certain catalytic RNA molecules (Ribozymes) act as catalysts.
TRAP 12 #12
❌ "Cohesion is water sticking to glass"
Truth: Water-to-glass is Adhesion. Water-to-water attraction is Cohesion.
