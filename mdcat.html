Methodology

Evidence-Based MDCAT Preparation

Four key pillars practiced by top merit rankers in DUHS, KEMU, and AKU entrance exams.

🧠

1. Active Recall

Do not rely on passive reading. Test yourself immediately after studying each textbook chapter through question banks.

🔁

2. Spaced Repetition

Review high-yield organic reactions, genetics ratios, and physics formulas on days 1, 3, 7, and 21 to build long-term memory.

📖

3. Textbook Primacy

PMDC questions are written directly from provincial textbooks (STBB, PCTB, NBF). Master every diagram, footnote, and table.

⚡

4. Timed Simulation

Acclimate your brain to 210 minutes of uninterrupted concentration to eliminate exam-day fatigue and panic.
